# SEP11 Freedom Project
by **Gary Huang**

## Context
I am a student at [HSTAT](https://www.hstat.org/) in the [Software Engineering Program](https://hstatsep.github.io/). The "Freedom Project" for SEP11 is a year-long project all about making something using JavaScript along with a third-party JS tool and you will need to present your freedom project.

For my project, I chose to independently study **Kaboom** in order to help me make **my collecting coins game**.

## Implications
### PROS
##### Global
  * Less reliance on aid and better finanical literacy
  * More savings and stronger financial stability
  * Less consumption can reduce waste

 ##### Social
 * Better money habits and less financial stress
 * Better financial planning
 * Encourages responsibility and financial awareness

 ##### Economic
 * creates jobs in game development
 * people save more money and improve finanical security
 * Encourages development

  
### CONS
##### Global
  * Less spending can mean lower tax revenue
  * Businesses may earn less if people less
  * The game's servers and use energy

 ##### Social
 * Can become too focused on saving
 * Different saving habits can create disagreements between people
 * May create pressure to save constantly

##### Economic
* Some industries may lose sales if people spend less
* Businesses may earn less if customers reduce spending
* Requires investment in servers
